Rewind privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026.

Rewind is a private time capsule for small groups. This policy says what Rewind stores, why, and how you delete it.

What we store

  • Your username, display name and a salted hash of your password. We never store the password itself. You sign up with a username and password only; Rewind does not ask for your email address or phone number.
  • The groups you create or join, and your role in each group.
  • The photos and videos you capture, including the sound in videos.
  • Your chat messages and reactions.
  • Reminder settings, the group time zone and, if you turn on reminders, a push subscription for your device.
  • Reports you make, members you block and moments a group owner removes.
  • Short-lived security records, such as hashed sign-in tokens and failed sign-in counts.

Why we store it

We use this data only to run Rewind: to sign you in, to share moments with the members of your groups, to make each group's film, to send the reminders you ask for, and to keep the service safe. Rewind has no ads. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or track you across other apps and websites.

Who can see it

Only the members of a group can see that group's moments, film and chat. The people who operate Rewind can access stored data to keep the service running and to act on reports. We review reports within 24 hours. When you block someone, their messages and moments are hidden from you.

Where it is stored

Photos, videos and group films are stored in private Amazon Web Services (AWS) S3 storage. Account details, group membership, chat and reminder settings are stored in the hosted database on AWS. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Web push reminders pass through your browser or device maker's push service.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists, unless you delete content sooner. Group films that have already been made stay with the group after account deletion. Unfinished uploads expire through scheduled cleanup and storage expiration rules; storage removal may take additional time after an upload expires. Recovery backups may contain earlier copies until they expire under the configured backup retention rules.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. Deletion is permanent. It removes your account, your sign-ins, your chat messages and reactions, your photos and videos, your reports and your blocks. Access is revoked when deletion completes; stored media is then removed through deletion and cleanup. A group you own passes to its longest-standing other member. A group with no other members is deleted with all its content. A group film that was already made with your moments stays with that group.

Children

Rewind is not for children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when this policy changes. For questions or requests, use the support page.
