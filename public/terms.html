Rewind terms of use

Effective 5 October 2026.

Rewind is a private time capsule for small groups. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

Your account

You sign up with a username and password. Keep your password private; you are responsible for what happens in your account. Rewind is not for children under 13.

What you share

You keep ownership of the photos, videos and messages you share. You let Rewind store them and show them to the members of your groups, including in each group's film.

No objectionable content or abuse

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Do not share:

  • Sexual content, nudity, or anything involving minors.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or discrimination.
  • Violent, graphic or illegal content, or content that encourages self-harm.
  • Photos or videos of people who have not agreed to be in them.
  • Spam, impersonation, or someone else's private information.

Reporting and blocking

You can report a message, a moment or a person from inside a group, and block a member so their messages and moments are hidden from you. A group owner can remove a moment from the group. We review reports within 24 hours.

Enforcement

If content or behaviour breaks these terms, we remove the content and may suspend or permanently terminate the account responsible, without notice.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time under Settings → Delete account. The privacy policy explains what is deleted.

The service

Rewind is provided as is. We may change or stop features, and we will update this page when these terms change.
