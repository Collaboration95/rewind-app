Rewind support

For help, to report a problem or abuse, or for a privacy request, open an issue at github.com/Collaboration95/rewind-app/issues. Do not include your password or private photos in an issue. We aim to reply within one business day.

Common questions

  • Report content or a person, or block a member: use the report and block options in the group. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that break the terms of use. Blocking hides that person's messages and moments from you.
  • Delete your account: in the app, open Settings → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what is deleted.
  • Forgot your password: open an issue with your username and we will help you get back in. Rewind accounts use a username and password only, with no email address or phone number.
