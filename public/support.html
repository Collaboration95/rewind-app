Rewind support

For help, to report a problem or abuse, or for a privacy request, open an issue at github.com/Collaboration95/rewind-app/issues. GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account to post. Do not include your password, sign-in tokens, private photos or videos, or other members' personal details in an issue. We aim to reply within one business day.

Common questions

  • Report content or a person, or block a member: use the report and block options in the group. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that break the terms of use. Blocking hides that person's messages and moments from you.
  • Delete your account: in the app, open Settings → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what is deleted.
  • Sign-in and recovery: web and Home Screen app sign-in uses Amazon Cognito Managed Login when configured. Use the recovery option on that sign-in page; recovery depends on the verified contact methods configured for your account. Rewind support cannot recover an account from a public username. Never post recovery codes.
  • Developer sign-in: the username/password form is for local and hosted development only, including the Android test app. These accounts are separate from Cognito accounts and have no self-service password recovery. It is not release sign-in.
  • Install on iPhone: open the HTTPS website in Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Rewind from its icon. Allow camera access for photos and camera and microphone access for videos. Enable reminders in Settings only if you want them; iPhone web push requires the installed Home Screen app on iOS 16.4 or later.
  • Camera or reminders blocked: check the browser and device permissions, then reopen Rewind. Capture needs a supported camera and a secure connection. Reminders depend on browser support, permission and connectivity; native test-app push is disabled.
