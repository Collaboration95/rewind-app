Rewind support

For help, to report a problem or abuse, or for a privacy request, open an issue at github.com/Collaboration95/rewind-app/issues. Do not include your password or private photos in an issue. We aim to reply within one business day.

Common questions

  • Report content or block a member: use the report and block options in the group.
  • Delete your account: use the delete-account option in the app. See the privacy policy for what is deleted.
  • Forgot your password: open an issue with your username and we will help you get back in.
